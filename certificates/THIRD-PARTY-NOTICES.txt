Mozilla CA certificate data
---------------------------
The included certificate bundle is a converted copy of Mozilla certificate
data obtained through the curl CA Extract service.

Source: https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html
Direct bundle: https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem
